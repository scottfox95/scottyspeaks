# SS2 0.3.11

Call recordings can now stop themselves. **Auto-stop after silence** (Call notes settings: Off, 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes; default 2) ends a recording once nobody has spoken for that long. It only arms after the first speech, so waiting in a lobby is safe. Speech is judged against each track's own background noise rather than a fixed volume.

Recordings that ran on after a call can be trimmed. **Trim** scans the saved audio, suggests where the talking stopped, and permanently cuts the audio after the chosen point. Transcribe the call again afterwards.

OpenRouter transcription no longer fails a whole call because one hour-long chunk of silence came back empty; that chunk is treated as no speech as long as another part produced text.
