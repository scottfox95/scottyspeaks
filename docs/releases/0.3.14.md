# SS2 0.3.14

## A failed paste leaves the text on your clipboard

When SS2 can't paste a dictation (the original field couldn't be confirmed, focus moved, or the keystroke failed), it still never pastes into a different field. But now the transcript **stays on your clipboard**, and a short notice says **"Paste didn't land, it's on your clipboard"**. Press ⌘V where you want it. The text is still saved in History as before.

Before, SS2 put your old clipboard back and told you to copy from History.

A successful paste works as before: your previous clipboard comes back a moment later.

## Folder names you can tell apart

When you file a call transcript, pinned folders now show their parent folder too, e.g. **TenenbaumAI / docs** and **ScottySpeaks / docs**, instead of several identical "docs". If two would still look the same, more of the path is shown (**Personal / Aravo / docs** vs **Work / Aravo / docs**). Hover for the full path. This applies to the folder picker, the pinned-folders list and the "saved to" line after filing.

Requires macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon). Installs automatically on Macs already running 0.3.13.
