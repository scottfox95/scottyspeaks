# SS2 0.3.13

**Recordings you can trust.** On November 9 a demo lost both of its recordings: the screen recording crashed as it started, and the backup call recording stopped itself 11 minutes in while people were still talking. Neither failure was obvious. This release fixes both and makes every unexpected stop loud.

## Call recordings no longer stop while people are talking

- Speech detection used a "noise floor" learned from the last 10 minutes of audio. On a talk-heavy call that floor *became* the talking, so normal speech stopped counting and auto-stop fired. Now anything louder than -40 dBFS always counts as activity, and the learned floor is held at or below -45 dBFS.
- **Auto-stop won't fire while Teams, Zoom, Webex, FaceTime, a Slack huddle or a browser call (Meet, Teams web) still has a call open.** SS2 checks the power assertion those apps hold during a call.
- **30-second warning.** Before an auto-stop, a notification and a bar on the Calls page say "Recording stops in 30 s", with a **Keep recording** button.
- **Default is now 10 minutes** (was 2). If you were on the old 2-minute default, you're moved to 10 once. If you chose something else, nothing changes. 15 and 30 minutes are new choices.
- When an auto-stop does happen, you get a notification.

## Screen recording no longer crashes in window mode

- Recording a single window crashed inside macOS if the window was minimized, on another Space, closed, or replaced (Teams does this when sharing starts). SS2 now checks the window first. If it isn't on screen, SS2 records the display it was on and tells you.
- **Entire screen** stays the default source. A note under the source picker explains the window fallback.

## Nothing fails silently

- Any stop you didn't ask for (a recorder crash, auto-stop, the disk-space guard, the 6-hour call limit, a start that never got going) now:
  - shows a macOS notification with the reason,
  - puts a ⚠ on the menu-bar icon (plus a "A recording stopped" menu item) until you dismiss it,
  - leaves a banner on the Calls or Screen recordings page until you dismiss it.
- **Watchdog:** while recording, SS2 checks every 2 seconds that the file is still growing. If it hasn't grown for 20 seconds you get the same alert. The recording isn't stopped, in case it recovers.
- The page shows **"Starting, checking audio is saving…"** until the file is actually seen growing, then "Recording".

## Record demo

A **Record demo** button on the Screen recordings page starts the whole screen (system audio + mic) **and** a call recording together. Both show their status side by side. If one fails, you're told and the other keeps going.

Requires macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon).
