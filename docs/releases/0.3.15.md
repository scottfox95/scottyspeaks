# SS2 0.3.15

## What you'll notice

**Dictation actually gets cleaned up now.** Since the first SS2 build, the AI polish step received only a short six-line instruction, not the full polish prompt (questions, lists, paragraphs, spoken commands, examples). On top of that, its safety checks threw away more than half of the edits it did make. So most dictations pasted exactly as the speech recognizer heard them: "looks exactly the same, as if nothing changed". Both are fixed:

- Replayed through the same model on your last 518 longer dictations, polish now changes **274** of them (it was **128**). Speed is the same (about a third of a second).
- **Question marks:** sentences built like a question ("Could we just…", "Do you think…, or should we…") now end in "?" **105 of 106** times (was 97 of 107). That includes short ones, which skip the AI: "Is this a question" gets its question mark too.
- It still won't put words in your mouth. If an edit drops or rewords more than about 15% of what you said, or changes a name, a number, a "not", or who's speaking ("my email" → "your email"), SS2 throws the edit away and pastes your words with light cleanup.

One limit: a question said as a statement ("You already sent it?") only gets a question mark if your voice rises clearly at the end. We tested a pitch detector on your recordings, and it got more wrong than right (you often end statements on a rise), so it's not included. Say "question mark" for those.

**Paste works in more apps.** SS2 now checks only that the app you were in is still there, in front and not in a password field. It no longer requires the exact same text field and cursor position. So it pastes into Slack, Claude, Notion, VS Code, Obsidian, Chrome and terminals, and clicking into the right field while you talk is fine. A huge clipboard (a screenshot) no longer blocks a paste.

**⌃⌥V pastes your last dictation** wherever the cursor is. Use it when a paste didn't land, or when you spoke first and clicked into the field afterwards. You can change or turn off the shortcut in Settings → Hotkeys.

**When a paste is refused, SS2 says why and where:** "Couldn't paste into Slack (a password field is active). Press ⌘V…" The text is on your clipboard.

**Your first words aren't cut off.** The microphone now opens at the same moment SS2 looks up where to paste, instead of after it. A slow app could hold up the mic by up to 2.5 seconds.

**No more previous dictation's text.** If a transcription ever timed out, every later dictation could paste the one before it until you restarted. Fixed.

**The polish model stays loaded.** Opening a window or changing models could unload it, so the next dictation waited for a 2.5 GB reload. SS2 now keeps the model you picked loaded and warms it at launch.

## A new dictation overlay

While you talk, the round button turns into a card in the same corner. It shows:
- **where the text is going** ("Into Slack");
- a **live level meter**, so you can see SS2 hears you, and a **timer**;
- a three-step progress line: listening, transcribing, polishing.

While polishing, **Paste raw now** pastes your words straight away without the AI pass.

When it's done, the card says **"Pasted into Slack ✓"** and fades. If a paste is refused, it says **why** and that the text is on your clipboard, and stays until you click **Got it**. Errors such as "microphone access is off" show on the card too, instead of a separate notice.

**Escape** cancels while you're talking or while it transcribes, and pastes the raw text while it polishes. SS2 only takes over Escape during a dictation; other apps get it back the moment it ends.

With "Reduce motion" on in macOS, the card only fades.

## Steadier calls and dictation

- **Calls:**
  - a quiet stretch on a call no longer fails the whole transcript;
  - a call that fails to start no longer breaks the call or blocks new takes;
  - low disk space stops a call cleanly, with an alert;
  - quitting waits for every recording to finish saving.
- **If SS2 crashes:** a damaged call list no longer stops SS2 from opening, and leftover model processes and temporary files are cleaned up at the next launch.
- **The hotkey:**
  - it no longer looks dead while a call is being transcribed;
  - it comes back on its own if its listener stops;
  - push-to-talk no longer shows a "install developer tools" prompt on a fresh Mac.
- **Cancelling while polishing** now pastes your words without the AI pass, instead of throwing them away.

## Simpler and local-first

- **A fresh Mac** uses on-device transcription and polish out of the box. It never points at the old OpenWhispr Cloud, which always failed with "requires sign-in". Your existing settings don't change.
- **Settings** opens on General. The Account, Plans & Billing and analytics screens are gone.
- **About 34,000 lines** of leftover code from the app SS2 was built from were removed: cloud sign-in, billing, Windows and Linux support. Nothing you use changes.
- SS2 no longer downloads fonts from Google when it opens.

## Safer updates

- **Publishing an update:** it refuses unless the code is committed and the tests pass. It also refuses if the build is signed by a different certificate, which would otherwise make every Mac ask for Microphone and Accessibility permission again.
- **The update script for other Macs** no longer needs Python. It checks the new app before swapping it in, and puts the old one back if anything fails.

## For troubleshooting

- Settings → Developer → Diagnostics shows **Recent pastes**: time, app, field type, pasted or refused and why. No text is stored.
- Warnings and errors always go to `logs/ss2.log` in SS2's data folder (rotates at 1 MB, keeps two old files). When polish is set aside, the reason is logged. Dictation text never is.

Requires macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon). Installs automatically on Macs running 0.3.13 or later.
