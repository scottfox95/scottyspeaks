1**Stop typing, start speaking.**

Wispr Flow lets you use effortless voice dictation in every application across your desktop and mobile devices. It's 4x faster than typing, supports AI commands, and auto-edits your messages!
1. 
2. **Open the app where you want to type**
    
    - Open any app with a text field, such as Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Docs, or Notion.
        
    - Click into a text box so you see the flashing cursor where you want Flow to type.
        
3. **Start your first dictation with the hotkey or mouse button**
    
    - Press and hold your **hotkey** or configured **mouse button**.
        
    - On Mac, the default hotkey is **Fn**.
        
    - You can also set a mouse button (such as Middle Click, Mouse4, or Mouse5) as your trigger in Flow settings. Mouse buttons work the same as keyboard shortcuts: hold for push-to-talk, or double-tap for hands-free mode. You can even combine a keyboard key with a mouse button (e.g., **Ctrl + Mouse4**).
        
    - When you hear Flow's ping or see the white bars moving, you can start speaking.
        
    - If you tap the hotkey or mouse button instead of holding it, you will see a **"Hold down [key], then speak"** toast notification with an animated key reminding you to hold down the shortcut while speaking, then release it when you are done.
        
    - If you have not clicked into a text box first, a **"Click the text box** 👉**"** toast will appear nudging you to focus a text field before dictating.
        
4. **Speak your message**
    
    - Speak naturally and at a steady pace.
        
    - Flow listens while you keep holding the hotkey or mouse button.
        
5. **Finish and paste your transcript**
    
    - Let go of the hotkey or mouse button when you are done speaking.
        
    - Flow will paste your formatted transcript into the text box where your cursor is.
        
6. **Cancel your dictation if needed**
    
    - Press **Esc** while dictating to cancel.
        
    - The text will not paste, but you can still find the transcript in **Recent activity** in Flow.
        
7. **Optional: start dictation from the Flow bar**
    
    - Click the **Flow bar** in the center of your screen.
        
    - Wait for the ping or moving white bars, then start speaking.
        
    - When you stop, Flow will paste your text into the active text field.
        
8. **Optional: use hands-free dictation**
    
    - Press **Fn + Space** to start hands-free mode, or double-tap your configured mouse button trigger.
        
    - Speak without holding any key or button.
        
    - When you are done, press **Fn** once (or tap your mouse button trigger once) to paste the transcript into the active text field.
        
9. **Optional: set up Mouse Flow with a guided banner**
    
    - If Flow detects you have a compatible external mouse (one with side buttons such as Mouse4/Mouse5), you may see a notification encouraging you to try **Mouse Flow**.
        
    - A dismissible discovery banner in the app will walk you through the setup: first bind a mouse button to Push-to-Talk or Hands-free mode, then optionally bind a second button to simulate pressing Enter after dictating.
        
    - To configure mouse button bindings, open **Settings** and go to **Keybind shortcuts** (previously called "Keyboard shortcuts"). From there you can assign buttons for Push-to-Talk, Hands-free, Command Mode, Paste, and the new **Enter Rebind**.
        
    - A red badge will appear in the Settings sidebar and next to **Keybind shortcuts** until your Mouse Flow setup is complete. It disappears once configured.
        
    - Mice with side buttons (M4/M5) work out of the box. Logitech MX-series mice require configuring **Logi Options+** first. Apple Magic Mouse and basic 3-button mice are not compatible.
